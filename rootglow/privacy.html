Rootglow – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

Rootglow ("the game") is made by Torjus Borg, Norway. This policy explains what data the game handles.

What we collect

We do not collect any personal data. The game has no account, no analytics and no server of its own.

What is stored on your device

Your game progress (charge, upgrades and settings) and which in-app purchases you own are stored locally on your device. This data never leaves your device through us.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect your device's advertising identifier, IP address, coarse location, device information and ad interaction data. On iOS 14.5 and later the game asks for your permission before your advertising identifier can be used for personalized ads (App Tracking Transparency). If you decline, you still see ads, but they are not personalized. You can change this at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.

Google's use of this data is covered by Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy and How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Rewarded ads are always optional. You can play the whole game without choosing to watch one.

In-app purchases

Purchases are handled entirely by Apple. We never see or store your payment details.

Children

The game is meant for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your rights

Because we do not hold any personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. For data held by Google, see Google's privacy policy. If you live in the EEA you can also contact your local data protection authority (in Norway: Datatilsynet).

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
