Refill

Privacy, simply.

Refill works without an account. Notes, app progress, settings and the last known Plus status stay on your phone. We do not run a server for this data.

Advertising

The free version shows a Google AdMob banner in Fuel and offers an optional rewarded video to unlock a recipe for one day. Google and its partners may process device identifiers, approximate location, ad interactions and diagnostic data to provide and measure ads. In regions where it is required, Google's consent form appears before ads. iOS also asks for App Tracking Transparency permission after an explanation. You can decline tracking and still use Refill. You can change ad privacy choices in Settings where available. See Google's Privacy Policy.

Purchases

Apple processes Refill Plus purchases, subscriptions and refunds. Refill reads your current StoreKit entitlement to unlock Plus and checks it again when the app starts. We do not receive your payment card details. Manage or cancel a subscription in your Apple account. See Apple's Privacy Policy.

Notifications

If you choose a reminder, the app schedules it locally on your device. You can turn it off in Settings or in iOS notification settings. We do not receive your notification schedule.

Voice guide

The optional voice guide plays audio files that ship inside the app, or uses your phone's built-in speech. Nothing you say or write is recorded or sent anywhere.

Your data

Refill does not require a login. Erase Refill data in Settings to remove locally stored notes and progress. This does not cancel a purchase or subscription; StoreKit can restore Plus.
