How can we help?

Where are my notes?

Your evening notes stay on this device. Refill does not use an account or sync them between devices.

How do I remove my data?

Open Settings and choose the option to delete your data. Removing the app also removes its locally stored data.

Why do I see ads?

The free version may show a small ad in Fuel. Watching a rewarded ad to unlock a recipe is optional. Refill Plus removes all ads.

How do I restore Refill Plus?

Open Refill Plus in the app and tap Restore purchases. Use the same Apple Account you bought it with.

How do I cancel a subscription?

Open Settings on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose Refill Plus. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

Is Refill medical care?

No. Refill offers short wellbeing routines and is not medical treatment. If you are in crisis, contact your local emergency services.
