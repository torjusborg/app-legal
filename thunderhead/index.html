Thunderhead – Support

Thunderhead is an idle storm game for iPhone. You are a thunderstorm: hold your finger on the sky and chain lightning through falling crystals.

Contact

Questions, bugs or feedback: torjusbok@gmail.com. We usually answer within a few days.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an internet connection?
No. The game works offline. Ads only load when you are online.

I bought "Remove Ads" on another device. How do I get it back?
Open Settings in the game and tap "Restore purchases", signed in with the same Apple ID.

Where is my progress saved?
On your device only. Deleting the app deletes your progress.

Can I turn off sound, vibration or screen shake?
Yes, all of them are in the in-game settings.
